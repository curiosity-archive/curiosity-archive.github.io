Hello there, and welcome!

First of all, thanks for stopping by and taking the time to explore this blog! Before you dive into any of the articles here, I thought it might be nice to share a little about why this place exists, what you can expect to find here, and where all these articles come from.

## Why Does This Place Exist?

I often find myself surrounded by things I know very little about, yet desperately want to understand. Beyond my own research, I have always been curious about how the world works, from the history of the universe and the nature of space and time to the fascinating machines people have built, the enormous engineering projects around the world, and the even more ambitious ones still on the planning board. There are just so many interesting things out there!

Unfortunately, curiosity is unlimited, but time and brainpower are not. For a long time, whenever an interesting question popped into my head, I rarely had enough time or energy to dig deeply into it. This was especially true before search engines became so powerful and AI tools became widely available. Quite often, I would end up with a vague understanding of something, along with an uncomfortable feeling that I was probably missing half the story. And, of course, finding the other half would require more time than I could spare.

Fortunately, technology has been making life a little easier for curious people (like me maybe). With the rapid development of AI tools in recent years, exploring unfamiliar subjects has become much less time-consuming and, frankly speaking, a lot more fun. I increasingly enjoy chatting with AI agents and asking them to put together interesting stories about whatever happens to catch my attention. 

Recently, whenever I need a break from my own research, I've found myself spending quite a bit of time reading these AI-generated stories, asking follow-up questions, and wandering from one fascinating topic to another. One question leads to the next, and before I know it, I've gone down yet another rabbit hole.

Then one day, a thought occurred to me: why not collect these articles somewhere?

It would be nice to have a place where I could revisit them later, instead of letting them disappear into the endless history of my AI conversations. And perhaps someone else, equally curious (or just sufficiently bored while wandering around the internet), might stumble upon this little collection and find something interesting to read.

And that's how this blog came to be. Nothing particularly grand or ambitious, just a little archive of curiosity.

## Where Do These Articles Come From?

Most of the articles you'll find here are generated with the help of AI agents. I read through all of them, and I sometimes ask multiple agents to cross-check the information or revise certain parts to make the stories clearer and more accurate.

That said, I mostly treat these articles as leisure reading rather than serious academic work. The topics cover a rather wide range of subjects, and I certainly don't claim to be an expert in all of them. (If I were, I suppose I should be writing research papers about these topics instead of asking AI to write introductory articles for me, haha..)

Although I try to make the articles as accurate and informative as possible, I can't guarantee that every detail is correct. AI can make mistakes, and so can I. So please bring your own curiosity, skepticism, and judgment when reading anything here. If something sounds surprising, questionable, or just too good to be true, I encourage you to dig a little deeper!

After all, questioning what we read is part of the fun of learning.

## One Last Thing...

This blog is not meant to be an authoritative encyclopedia, a serious academic publication, or a place where every question comes with a definitive answer. Think of it more as a collection of interesting stories and explorations, put together by someone who enjoys learning about things far beyond his own field.

If you find something interesting here, learn a little something new, or end up with even more questions than you started with, then I think this little place has served its purpose.

Thanks again for visiting, and I hope you enjoy wandering around!

Stay curious, keep exploring, and never get bored!
